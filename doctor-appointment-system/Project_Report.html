Executive Summary

The MediConnect project is a sophisticated web-based healthcare ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between patients, medical professionals, and emergency services. This report documents the technical architecture, implemented modules, and final verification results of the system.

Technical Architecture

Authentication & Security System (authController.js & AuthContext.jsx)

Handles secure, multi-role access for Patients, Doctors, and Admins. Features OTP-based verification for patients and a streamlined email-authentication flow for healthcare professionals.

Appointment Booking Engine (appointmentController.js & DoctorSearch.jsx)

Facilitates real-time slot availability, booking confirmations, and automated status transitions from pending to accepted with doctor-specific filters.

Emergency SOS & Ambulance Tracker (Ambulance.jsx & ambulanceController.js)

Provides instant ambulance booking with live GPS tracking and realistic route simulation following street-like paths.

Live Token & Queue Management (LiveToken.jsx & LiveToken.js)

Reduces hospital waiting times by providing patients with a live-queue status and real-time token tracking.
